# BluSlate

**Got a business idea? Find out if it will work, before you spend real money.**

BluSlate walks a founder from a rough idea to a tested plan. You answer guided questions, see the numbers update as you go (market size, unit cost, runway), and finish with pitch documents you can download and share.

👉 **Try it:** https://bluslate.yoppi.in (pilot, free; sign in with your email, or try it as a guest)

## How it works

Seven steps, each usable on its own, each feeding the next:

1. **Problem Discovery:** is the problem real, and who has it?
2. **Customer Discovery:** what do real customers say?
3. **Market Research:** how big is the market?
4. **Product Design:** what exactly will you build first?
5. **Operations Setup:** what does it cost to make and deliver?
6. **Go-to-Market Plan:** how will customers find you, and what does each one cost?
7. **Launch & Growth:** how long does the money last, and when do you break even?

Every step ends with a short report. Then **Pitch docs** turns your work into five documents: a one-page summary, a pitch deck, a business plan, a Business Model Canvas and a financial model.

## Where it stands

**Ready now (pilot):**
- All seven steps work, with live goals and a report at the end of each. Goals turn green as you complete them.
- Steps listed on the left, a Passport at the top right that stamps each finished step, and a Skip button on every step.
- Answer something once: shared answers (who the customer is, where you'll launch, price, costs) fill in the other steps for you.
- Pitch docs: generate and download each document (PDF, or Excel for the financial model) whenever you like.
- Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email. Your startups are saved to your account and open on any device.
- Or continue as a guest with just your name and email; sign in later and keep your work.
- The seven-step circle on the Home page: press Start, or try any single step, then Pitch after step 7.
- Your Idea Book, friends (Collaborate), Consult with the team, and Feedback, from the menu under your name.
- An admin dashboard for the team, with visitor analytics and where people drop off.
